    How to use it
    • Right and left mean the way the wire turns as you walk along it from end A toward end B, with the wire lying on the template, printed side up.
    • Mark at is the center of each bend. Put the middle of your bending jaw or mandrel on the mark. The thin ticks on the template show where each bend starts and ends.
    • Cut length follows the wire's center, so it already allows for the material that goes around each bend.
    • Copper springs back a little. Bend a hair past the angle, then lay the wire on the template and adjust.
    • Printing: choose Actual size or 100% (not “Fit to page”), then check the 1-inch square with a ruler. Big shapes print on several sheets: overlap them so the 1-inch grid and dashed lines line up, and tape.
    • DXF is the centerline with true arcs, in inches, for SolidWorks or a CAD drawing.
    • Your projects are saved on this device only. Nothing is sent anywhere.
