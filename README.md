# Claude-Apps

Small web apps built with Claude, served with GitHub Pages.

- [Knot of the Day](https://dfwilley.github.io/Claude-Apps/knot-of-the-day/) — learn one sailor's knot at a time with step-by-step drawings.
- [Wire Bender](https://dfwilley.github.io/Claude-Apps/wire-bender/) — draw a copper wire shape and get a full-size printable template plus a step-by-step bend list (also SVG and DXF downloads).
- [Blue Book](https://dfwilley.github.io/Claude-Apps/blue-book/) — shelved November 23, 2026; still works but is no longer being developed. Photograph your Navy flight logbook pages (or import a scanned PDF, one logbook page per PDF page) and get a searchable record of your career: career totals, a timeline of squadrons, aircraft and deployments, and Excel, CSV and PDF exports, plus a file made for MyFlightbook's Import page. Everything stays on your device unless you switch on the optional AI reader: the built-in reader copes with print, not handwriting, so the web app can instead send a picture of each page to Claude (Anthropic) using your own key. On a computer (Chrome or Edge) the logbook can be kept in a folder you choose, so two computers can share one copy. iPhone app via TestFlight, or [Blue-Book.ipa](https://github.com/dfwilley/Claude-Apps/releases/download/blue-book-latest/Blue-Book.ipa) (install with Sideloadly).
- [HGVC Binder](https://dfwilley.github.io/Claude-Apps/hgvc-binder/) — Hilton Grand Vacations ownership in one place: a tab per property with a Points Wallet (balances, saved/borrowed points, expirations, reservations), a Fee Tracker (yearly fees, increases chart, cost per point, paid/unpaid) and an Ownership Binder (contract summary, statements and deeds, phone numbers). Reminders before points expire and fees come due. Reads a data file from your Dropbox; your data never goes to GitHub. iPhone app: [HGVC-Binder.ipa](https://github.com/dfwilley/Claude-Apps/releases/download/hgvc-binder-latest/HGVC-Binder.ipa) (install with Sideloadly; adds Face ID for account numbers and phone reminders).
- [Bin Finder](https://dfwilley.github.io/Claude-Apps/bin-finder/) — photograph what's in each storage bin, shelf or RV compartment, then type or say what you're looking for and it shows which bin, with the photo. QR labels, trip packing checklist, backups. iPhone app: [Bin-Finder.ipa](https://github.com/dfwilley/Claude-Apps/releases/download/bin-finder-latest/Bin-Finder.ipa) (install with Sideloadly).
- [Ask Me Tomorrow](https://dfwilley.github.io/Claude-Apps/ask-me-tomorrow/) — one question a day about your life; answer out loud, and the answers build into a memoir for the family, sorted into chapters. Web app (Share › Add to Home Screen).
- [The Job Jar!](https://dfwilley.github.io/Claude-Apps/job-jar/) — a picture chore chart for kids about 4 to 8. Each child taps their own animal, then taps a picture when a chore is done to earn a star (with a chime, and the chore read out loud for kids who can't read yet). Stars fill their jar and buy rewards the family picks; a grown-up PIN OKs rewards and can check chores first. Kids can use their own photo as their picture. Money mode: pick one coin (penny to dollar coin) and how many each chore pays, shown as a stack of coins. Week chart, history, backups. Everything stays on the device. In the iPhone/iPad app, a kid's own device can be linked to a grown-up's device with a 6-number code: the kid's device shows only that kid's chart, and the two stay in step over the home Wi-Fi (no internet or cloud). iPhone/iPad app via TestFlight, or [Job-Jar.ipa](https://github.com/dfwilley/Claude-Apps/releases/download/job-jar-latest/Job-Jar.ipa) (install with Sideloadly).
- [Who Was Here](https://dfwilley.github.io/Claude-Apps/who-was-here/) — plan a drive, flight (airport codes) or cruise (ports in order), or just use where you are, and get the famous people (born, lived, died or buried there), historic events and battles, landmarks and museums, and aviation and Navy history along the way, sorted by how far each is off your route or by mile along the way. Map, ★ picks, share or print your list; trips are saved on the device. Data from Wikidata/Wikipedia and OpenStreetMap. Web app (Share › Add to Home Screen).
- [Gate Time Puzzles](https://dfwilley.github.io/Claude-Apps/gate-time-puzzles/) — one-minute brain teasers in math, navigation and mechanics for airport waits. Tap to answer; each category levels up as you improve (Student Pilot to Top Gun), every answer shows how to work it, and results land on an LSO-style Greenie Board. Optional boarding countdown. Puzzles are made on the phone, so it works with no signal. Web app (Share › Add to Home Screen).
- [Gear Bag Check](https://dfwilley.github.io/Claude-Apps/gear-bag-check/) — a packing checklist for each sport, for kids to use at the door. Each player has their own bags (starter lists for soccer, baseball, softball and basketball, or make your own) with the gear, uniform and water bottles that go in. Tap each picture as it goes in the bag; "Ready to go" says Cleared for takeoff or Hold short with what's still missing. A ! on any item reports it lost, worn out or too small, and grown-ups (press and hold) see one Needs attention list across all the kids, plus recent door checks. A Hair section with a one-tap hair kit (hair ties, gel, slick-back brush). Each player picks their own style (Field, Berry, Midnight, or Custom with a color wheel for their own colors), one of 12 fonts, and any emoji (a big picker or type your own) and Fun or Simple wording, and has a schedule with a small calendar: practices and games (one-time or weekly), each tied to a bag, so the right bag shows "Today". US holidays show on the calendars (can be turned off in Settings), and the main page has a family calendar with everyone's plans plus its own color and font. Plans can have a place (saved places to pick again), and a Next up card counts down to the next game. A Pre-game screen has a one-minute breathing timer; players who turn on Faith also get a Bible verse of the day (Berean Standard Bible, public domain) and short pre-game prayers. Practice days show "Today", items can be read out loud, and backups save to a file. Everything stays on the device. Web app (Share › Add to Home Screen).
- [Squadron Flashback](https://dfwilley.github.io/Claude-Apps/squadron-flashback/) — one random question a day about your Navy flying days: a hard lesson learned, a person who shaped you, a landing you still remember. Type the answer or dictate it (the microphone on the iPhone keyboard, or a Dictate button where the browser offers one) and save it to a growing journal. 136 questions in 12 chapters, from Earning the Wings to Looking Back; swap a question for a different one, skip one for good, add your own, or write a story with no question at all. The journal can be searched, sorted by chapter and edited, and Share makes one file with every story that opens in any browser and prints as a book for the grandchildren. Daily Calendar reminder, backups to a file. Works with no signal; everything stays on the device. Web app (Share › Add to Home Screen).
- [Sketch Match](https://dfwilley.github.io/Claude-Apps/sketch-match/) — drawing practice. Pick one of 19 line drawings (easy, medium, tricky or expert, with a new "Today's sketch" each day), copy it on paper with the help of starting steps and an optional grid, then take a photo of the drawing. A coach (Claude, an AI) compares the photo with the picture and gives stars, what went well, what to try next and a small challenge. Past tries are kept in a Sketchbook on the device. On weekdays the app can be used for 20 minutes a day (weekends have no limit); a parent with the PIN can add time, lift the limit for a day, or change it in Setup. Checking a drawing sends the photo to the Claude API through the family's own small Cloudflare worker script, which holds the API key (setup in `sketch-match/worker/SETUP.md`); nothing else leaves the device. Web app (Share › Add to Home Screen).
- [Brick Pile Builder](https://dfwilley.github.io/Claude-Apps/brick-pile/) — shelved November 21, 2026; still works but is no longer being developed. Take a picture of your loose LEGO-style bricks and get a list of what you have, which you can fix with − and +, by tapping a line to say what the piece really is, or by adding more pictures. Then ask what you can build: three ideas at a time, for a little kid, a kid or a grown-up, each with the pieces it uses and layer-by-layer directions. It works with nothing to set up: the bricks are counted on the device by colour and rough size (small, medium, large), and the ideas come from 32 built-in builds drawn in your own colours; nothing leaves the device and it works with no signal. Optionally, with your own Anthropic key, Claude looks at each picture instead and names the pieces (2 x 4 brick, roof slope, wheel) and writes ideas for your exact pile, at a few cents a picture. Not connected with the LEGO Group. iPhone app via TestFlight, or [Brick-Pile.ipa](https://github.com/dfwilley/Claude-Apps/releases/download/brick-pile-latest/Brick-Pile.ipa) (install with Sideloadly).
