The Job Jar! Privacy Policy

In effect from November 20, 2026

The short version: The Job Jar! collects nothing. Everything you put in it stays on your own devices.

This applies to the iPhone and iPad app and to the web version at dfwilley.github.io/Claude-Apps/job-jar/.

What the app keeps, and where

To work as a chore chart, the app saves what a grown-up or child types or picks: children's first names or nicknames, a picture for each child (an animal or a photo you choose), chores, rewards, special days, matching offers, and the stars or money earned and spent.

All of that is saved only on the device, inside the app (or inside your browser, for the web version). The developer cannot see it and never receives it.

What the app does not do

  • No accounts and no sign-in.
  • No advertising.
  • No tracking, analytics or crash reporting.
  • No in-app purchases.
  • No links to other apps or websites in the children's screens.
  • It does not send your information over the internet.

Photos

If you choose a photo for a child's picture, the app keeps a small copy on the device. It is not uploaded anywhere. The app does not look through your photo library; it only receives the one picture you pick.

Linking a kid's device to a grown-up's device

In the iPhone and iPad app, a grown-up can link a child's device to their own with a 6-number code. The two devices then share that child's chart directly with each other over your home Wi-Fi. Nothing goes through the internet or through any server, and the developer has no part in it. The first time, iOS asks for permission to find devices on your local network; that is what this is for.

Backups

A grown-up can save a backup file. The file goes wherever you choose to save or send it, and is yours to keep or delete.

Children

The Job Jar! is made for families with young children. It does not collect personal information from children or from anyone else. Grown-up settings and rewards are behind a grown-up PIN.

Removing your information

Use Grown-ups › Settings › Erase everything, or delete the app. For the web version, clearing the site's data in your browser removes it. Backup files you saved stay where you put them until you delete them.

The web version

The web version is a set of pages hosted by GitHub Pages. Like any website host, GitHub may record technical details such as your device's internet address when the pages load. The Job Jar! itself does not receive or use that information. See GitHub's privacy statement.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about privacy or about the app can be sent through the project's page on GitHub: github.com/dfwilley/Claude-Apps/issues.
