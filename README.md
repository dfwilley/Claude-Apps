# Claude-Apps

Small web apps built with Claude, served with GitHub Pages.

- [Knot of the Day](https://dfwilley.github.io/Claude-Apps/knot-of-the-day/) — learn one sailor's knot at a time with step-by-step drawings.
- [Wire Bender](https://dfwilley.github.io/Claude-Apps/wire-bender/) — draw a copper wire shape and get a full-size printable template plus a step-by-step bend list (also SVG and DXF downloads).
- [Blue Book](https://dfwilley.github.io/Claude-Apps/blue-book/) — photograph your Navy flight logbook pages and get a searchable record of your career: career totals, a timeline of squadrons, aircraft and deployments, and Excel, CSV and PDF exports. Everything stays on your phone. iPhone app: [Blue-Book.ipa](https://github.com/dfwilley/Claude-Apps/releases/download/blue-book-latest/Blue-Book.ipa) (install with Sideloadly).
- [HGVC Binder](https://dfwilley.github.io/Claude-Apps/hgvc-binder/) — Hilton Grand Vacations ownership in one place: a tab per property with a Points Wallet (balances, saved/borrowed points, expirations, reservations), a Fee Tracker (yearly fees, increases chart, cost per point, paid/unpaid) and an Ownership Binder (contract summary, statements and deeds, phone numbers). Reminders before points expire and fees come due. Reads a data file from your Dropbox; your data never goes to GitHub. iPhone app: [HGVC-Binder.ipa](https://github.com/dfwilley/Claude-Apps/releases/download/hgvc-binder-latest/HGVC-Binder.ipa) (install with Sideloadly; adds Face ID for account numbers and phone reminders).
- [Bin Finder](https://dfwilley.github.io/Claude-Apps/bin-finder/) — photograph what's in each storage bin, shelf or RV compartment, then type or say what you're looking for and it shows which bin, with the photo. QR labels, trip packing checklist, backups. iPhone app: [Bin-Finder.ipa](https://github.com/dfwilley/Claude-Apps/releases/download/bin-finder-latest/Bin-Finder.ipa) (install with Sideloadly).
- [Ask Me Tomorrow](https://dfwilley.github.io/Claude-Apps/ask-me-tomorrow/) — one question a day about your life; answer out loud, and the answers build into a memoir for the family, sorted into chapters. Web app (Share › Add to Home Screen).
- [The Job Jar!](https://dfwilley.github.io/Claude-Apps/job-jar/) — a picture chore chart for kids about 4 to 8. Each child taps their own animal, then taps a picture when a chore is done to earn a star (with a chime, and the chore read out loud for kids who can't read yet). Stars fill their jar and buy rewards the family picks; a grown-up PIN OKs rewards and can check chores first. Kids can use their own photo as their picture. Money mode: pick one coin (penny to dollar coin) and how many each chore pays, shown as a stack of coins. Week chart, history, backups. Everything stays on the device. In the iPhone/iPad app, a kid's own device can be linked to a grown-up's device with a 6-number code: the kid's device shows only that kid's chart, and the two stay in step over the home Wi-Fi (no internet or cloud). iPhone/iPad app via TestFlight, or [Job-Jar.ipa](https://github.com/dfwilley/Claude-Apps/releases/download/job-jar-latest/Job-Jar.ipa) (install with Sideloadly).
- [Who Was Here](https://dfwilley.github.io/Claude-Apps/who-was-here/) — plan a drive, flight (airport codes) or cruise (ports in order), or just use where you are, and get the famous people (born, lived, died or buried there), historic events and battles, landmarks and museums, and aviation and Navy history along the way, sorted by how far each is off your route or by mile along the way. Map, ★ picks, share or print your list; trips are saved on the device. Data from Wikidata/Wikipedia and OpenStreetMap. Web app (Share › Add to Home Screen).
- [Gate Time Puzzles](https://dfwilley.github.io/Claude-Apps/gate-time-puzzles/) — one-minute brain teasers in math, navigation and mechanics for airport waits. Tap to answer; each category levels up as you improve (Student Pilot to Top Gun), every answer shows how to work it, and results land on an LSO-style Greenie Board. Optional boarding countdown. Puzzles are made on the phone, so it works with no signal. Web app (Share › Add to Home Screen).
