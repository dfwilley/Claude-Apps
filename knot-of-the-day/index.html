    About this app

    One knot a day, picked from the knots you’re ready for. A knot is “ready” once you’ve learned the knots it builds on. Tap the drawing (or Next) to go step by step: the red dashed arrow shows the next move.

    Your progress and notes stay on this phone. Nothing is sent anywhere.
