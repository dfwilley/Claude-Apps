Share and settings

For the grandchildren

Share the journal

Makes one file with every story, sorted into chapters. It opens in any web browser on a phone or computer, and prints as a book.

Daily reminder

Adds a repeating event to your iPhone Calendar that reminds you each day. Tap Add All when the Calendar asks.

Back up your stories

Your stories are stored only on this phone, in this app. Back up now and then (save the file to iCloud Drive or Dropbox) so nothing is lost if the phone is replaced.

Install on your Home Screen

In Safari, tap the Share button, then Add to Home Screen. It then opens like a regular app, and the iPhone is much less likely to clear its storage.

Dictating

Tap in the answer box, then tap the microphone at the bottom of the iPhone keyboard and talk. Say "period" or "new paragraph" as you go. Where the browser offers it, a Dictate button also appears under the answer box.
